# Deadline with a countdown

A mentor has until 12 hours before the session to decide (R05, D07). The pattern says when, how soon, and when it becomes urgent – without a ticking clock.

<Canvas of={Stories.UnderTwoHours} />

## Rules

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Show the deadline as a time with its zone and a relative phrase: "Decide by Tue 27 Oct, 05:00 (Europe/Warsaw, UTC+1) · in 8 h". | spec E02, C12 |
| The page holds the clock (`useNow`) and refreshes it once a minute; no second-by-second countdown. | C12, P10 |
| Under two hours add the words "Decide soon" and an icon. Colour or weight alone never carries urgency. | C12, WCAG 1.4.1 |
| Past the deadline the text says "Deadline passed"; the server refuses an acceptance (`conflict`) even before the sweep. | spec E02 (Edge Cases) |
| The accent goes to *Accept* of the nearest deadline only. | Q3, P5/K3 |
| `now` is a prop, so stories and tests are deterministic; `useNow(start)` fixes the first reading and runs on from it. | C2 |
| General form: `VERB by TIME, ZONE · RELATIVE`, the verb is a parameter (*Decide by …*, *Free cancellation until … · in 8 h*). Under two hours the same words and icon apply. Past the deadline the text says it has passed, without a relative time. | agent test 2026-10-20 (lekcja-06/test-agentem.md), gap 13 |
| No email or push: the in-app notice (`PendingBookingsNotice`) is the signal. | D15 |
