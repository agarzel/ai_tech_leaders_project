# E02 – Booking and prepayment

Backlog #22 with stories #29–#32, specified in `2026-10-19-booking-and-prepayment.md`. A signed-in developer picks a slot and a length, writes the question, pays by card, and waits for the mentor, who accepts or declines until 12 hours before the session. A decline or a missed deadline is a full refund.

## Flow

| Step | Screen | What happens | Components | State map |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/m/[slug]` | The mentee picks a slot and a length; the price is in the button | `SlotList`, `SlotBookActions` | [Screens](?path=/docs/product-e02-screens-state-map--docs) |
| 2 | `/book/[slotId]` | Summary, question (≤ 2,000), note that the mentor may decline, *Pay* | `BookingForm`, `BookingSummary`, `QuestionField` | same |
| 3 | hosted card payment page | Outside the design system (Stripe Checkout; `/dev/fake-checkout` locally) | – | – |
| 4 | `/bookings/[id]` | Back from payment: *Payment processing…*, then the booking's status and next action | `PaymentPending`, `BookingStatusPanel`, `BookingSummary`, `BookingQuestion` | same |
| 5 | `/mentor` | The mentor sees how many bookings wait and by when | `PendingBookingsNotice` | same |
| 6 | `/mentor/bookings` | The mentor reads the question, accepts or declines (reason ≤ 280) | `MentorBookingRow`, `AcceptDeadline`, `DeclineBookingDialog`, `BookingQuestion` | same |
| 7 | `/bookings` | The mentee sees all bookings by status | `BookingRow`, `BookingStatusBadge`, `ActiveFilters`, `ResultCount`, `Pagination` | same |

## The booking state machine in the interface

| Booking | Payment | Mentee reads | Mentor reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pending_payment` | `open` | Payment processing… | – (not shown) |
| `awaiting_acceptance` | `paid` | Waiting for {mentor} — they decide by … | Waiting for your decision — decide by … |
| `confirmed` | `paid` | Confirmed | Confirmed |
| `declined` | `refund_requested` / `refunded` / `refund_failed` | Declined — refund on its way / Refunded / Refund delayed — we are on it | You declined this booking |
| `expired` | same | Expired — refund on its way / Refunded / Refund delayed | This booking expired |
| `payment_expired` | `expired` (or a late payment, refunded) | Payment not completed — the time was released | same |

All wording lives in `describeBooking` and `bookingStatusLabel`.

## Decisions that shape the screens

| Decision | Effect |
|---|---|
| D16: card only | one payment method line, no method choice |
| 35-minute hold | stated on the booking form; the page after payment waits for the webhook |
| Price stored at booking | the UI shows the stored amount, never computes one |
| D05: the mentor reads the question first | the question is on every mentor row; declining is a normal outcome |
| D15: no email for mentors | `PendingBookingsNotice` is the signal |
| Max 2 unpaid bookings | the refusal is explained on the form |
| Mentee by name only | `MentorBooking` has no email |
