# Shape and depth

Small radii, thin lines, no shadows. Depth comes from surfaces (`background` → `card`) and 1 px borders, not from elevation.

<Unstyled>
  <ShapeScale />
</Unstyled>

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| `--radius` is 6 px; use the `rounded-*` scale. Never larger than `rounded-xl`, except `rounded-full` for avatars. | T7, A01/P13 |
| Rows are separated by a 1 px `border-border` line; a list has one frame of the same weight. | R01.3 |
| No box in a box: a panel never contains another framed panel. | R01.3/P3 |
| No shadows on lists, cards or panels. Overlays (menu, popover, dialog) are separated by their `popover` surface and 1 px border. | R01.3, tokeny.md (no shadow tokens) |
| No glow, no glass (`backdrop-blur`), no gradients. | A01 |
