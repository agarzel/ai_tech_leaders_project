## Density

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Lists and panels are dense: rows of `py-list-row px-list-row`, separated by a 1 px line, many rows visible without scrolling. | R01.1, K1 |
| The session view is the exception: the conversation column is the main element, with minimal metadata. | K1, R02.1 |
| Controls are 32 px high (`h-control`); on touch screens (`pointer: coarse`) 40 px. Every click target is at least 24 × 24 px. | P4, WCAG 2.5.8 |

## Layout widths

Check every screen at **320, 768 and 1440 px** (toolbar → viewport).

| Width | What changes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 320 px | One column; panels full width; the gutter is `p-panel` (16 px); no horizontal scroll. | WCAG 1.4.10 |
| 768 px (`md`) | Side panels (e.g. session context) may sit next to the main column. | proposal |
| 1440 px | Content stays in a centred column of at most `max-w-5xl` (1024 px); running text at most `max-w-prose`. | **proposal – to approve** |
