# AppHeader

`import { AppHeader } from "@devmentor/ui/backend/layout/AppHeader"`

Panel block: word mark + `RoleNav` + account area (`AccountMenu`).

## 1. Purpose

Tells a person where they are and who they are signed in as, on every page, with the way to their role's main places.

## 2. When to use

| Situation in DevMentor | Source |
|---|---|
| The top of every page, inside `PageLayout` | spec E01, UI/UX ("Header shows Log in / Register, or the user's name and Log out") |

## 3. When not to use

| Instead of `AppHeader`… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Inside the session view, a side panel of session context | the session panel (E03 – not built, "What is missing") | R02.6, K4 |
| A marketing hero | nothing | A01 (hero + three cards) |

## 4. Anatomy

1. Word mark *DevMentor* (link home; the admin home for admins).
2. `RoleNav` (optional; none on sign-in pages).
3. Account area (`AccountMenu`, optional).

A 1 px line under it, no shadow (R01.3).

## 5. Variants

None. Content per role:

<Canvas of={AppHeaderStories.PerRole} />

## 6. States

States belong to its parts: links (`RoleNav` – default, hover, focus, current), account (`AccountMenu` – signed out, loading, signed in, logging out). The header itself has no state.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A `header` → `banner` landmark, once per page. | WAI-ARIA landmarks |
| Navigation is a named `nav` (`navLabel`, default *Main*). | WCAG 2.4.1 |
| Tab order: word mark, navigation, account. | WCAG 2.4.3 |

<Canvas of={AppHeaderStories.Landmarks} />

## 8. Content

The word mark is the product name only – no tagline, no logo square (A01, audit). Long names in the account area are truncated (`title` keeps the full name).

<Canvas of={AppHeaderStories.LongName} />

## 9. Responsive

Up to 1024 px of content, aligned with the page (F2). At 320 px the navigation wraps under the word mark.

<Canvas of={AppHeaderStories.Narrow} />

## 10. API

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `homeHref` | `string` | `"/"` | word mark target |
| `nav` | `RoleNavItem[]` | `[]` | role navigation |
| `currentHref` | `string` | – | current item |
| `navLabel` | `string` | `"Main"` | navigation landmark name |
| `account` | `ReactNode` | – | `AccountMenu` |
| `renderLink` | `(props) => ReactNode` | plain `<a>` | Next.js `Link` in the app (C29) |

## 12. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| A line under the header. | A shadow or a blurred, translucent bar (A01). |
| Only the role's places. | Every page of the product for everyone. |

## 13. Related

- **Confused with:** `PageLayout` (the whole frame), `RoleNav` (only the links).
- **Used in:** every screen (`Product/E01/Screens`).
