# PaymentPending

`import { PaymentPending } from "@devmentor/ui/components/bookings/PaymentPending"`

Product component, concept `payments` (E02, #30). Instance of the pattern [Payment and waiting](?path=/docs/patterns-payment-and-waiting--docs).

## 1. Purpose

Tells the mentee the payment is being confirmed, and after about a minute that the page will update shortly.

## 2. When to use

| Situation in DevMentor | Source |
|---|---|
| `/bookings/[id]` after the card payment, until the webhook lands | spec E02, UI/UX |

## 3. When not to use

| Instead of `PaymentPending`… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting for the mentor | `BookingStatusPanel` | a decision, not a payment |

## 4. Anatomy

A titled section: title (with a spinner while checking), one sentence in a polite status.

## 5. Variants

`checking` and `slow`.

| State | Story |

The checking itself – every few seconds for up to a minute – is the page's job (see the screen stories).

<Canvas of={Stories.Slow} />

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| The spinner is decorative; the sentence is a polite status. | WCAG 4.1.3 |
| After a minute the spinner stops: nothing spins forever. | C66, WCAG 2.2.2 |
| Nothing is asked of the person. | R01.5 |

## 8. Content

"Payment processing…", "This page will update shortly". No "please do not close this page" – the status is stored on the server.
