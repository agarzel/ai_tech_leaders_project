# E02 screens – state map

Each screen state has its own full-screen story (C14). This batch covers booking and payment: the E02 form of `/m/[slug]`, `/book/[slotId]` and `/bookings/[id]`. Batch 6 adds the lists and the mentor's decisions. The hosted card payment page and `/dev/fake-checkout` are outside the design system (C13).

| State | Story |
|---|---|

| A visitor who is not signed in is sent to `/login?next=…` and returns (app behaviour, no story) | – |
