# E02 screens – state map

Each screen state has its own full-screen story (C14). This batch covers booking and payment: the E02 form of `/m/[slug]`, `/book/[slotId]` and `/bookings/[id]`. The mentor's decisions, `/bookings` and `/mentor/bookings` follow in batch 6. The hosted card payment page and `/dev/fake-checkout` are outside the design system (C13).

## /m/[slug] with booking (#29)

| State | Story |
|---|---|
| Each slot offers *Book 25 min · price* and *Book 50 min · price*; no accent | [WithBooking](?path=/story/product-e01-screens-mentorpage--with-booking) |
| A visitor who is not signed in is sent to `/login?next=…` and returns (app behaviour, no story) | – |
