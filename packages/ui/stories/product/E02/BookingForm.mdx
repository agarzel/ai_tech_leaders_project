# BookingForm

`import { BookingForm } from "@devmentor/ui/components/bookings/BookingForm"`

Product component, concept `bookings` (E02, #30). Instance of the pattern [Form](?path=/docs/patterns-form--docs) and [Payment and waiting](?path=/docs/patterns-payment-and-waiting--docs). A bespoke form: `CrudForm` has no summary, no counter and no payment step (AGENTS.md: say why).

## 1. Purpose

Turns the chosen slot and a question into a booking and sends the mentee to pay.

## 2. When to use

| Situation in DevMentor | Source |
|---|---|
| `/book/[slotId]` | spec E02, UI/UX |

## 3. When not to use

| Instead of `BookingForm`… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Paying | the hosted card payment page | no card data reaches DevMentor (D16) |
| Changing a booking | not built | a mentee cannot cancel before acceptance (spec E02, R04 is #34) |

## 4. Anatomy

*Your booking* (`BookingSummary`), `QuestionField`, *Payment* (card payment, 35-minute hold, the mentor may decline – full refund), *Pay \{price\}* (the accent) with a status line.

## 5. Variants

None; length and price come from the summary.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Values kept after an error; one submit at a time. | Form pattern |
| After success the button stays busy until the browser leaves – no second booking by double click. | C65 |
| Focus goes to the question on a field error and to the error message otherwise. | WCAG 2.4.3 |
| The refusal is in words and says what to do (taken, finish the previous payment, nothing was charged). | spec E02 (Edge Cases) |
| One accent: *Pay*. | R01.5, P5/K3 |

## 8. Content

Button *Pay PLN 180.00* – verb and amount. The note says the mentor may decline and the refund is full; the hold is 35 minutes; payment is by card.
