# BookingStatusPanel

`import { BookingStatusPanel } from "@devmentor/ui/components/bookings/BookingStatusPanel"`

Product component, concept `bookings` (E02, #30, #32). Instance of the pattern [Status and next action](?path=/docs/patterns-status-and-next-action--docs).

## 1. Purpose

Says where a booking stands and what happens next, above the fold of its page.

## 2. When to use

| Situation in DevMentor | Source |
|---|---|
| `/bookings/[id]` for the mentee | spec E02, UI/UX |
| The mentor's view of a booking (batch 6) | spec E02, UI/UX |

## 3. When not to use

| Instead of `BookingStatusPanel`… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A row in a list | `BookingStatusBadge` | one phrase is enough |
| Payment progress | `PaymentPending` (used inside this panel) | a different state with its own timing |

## 4. Anatomy

A titled section: title (the state), one sentence, the mentor's reason (to the mentee only), the next action.

## 5. Variants

`audience`: *mentee* or *mentor*. Wording per state is in `describeBooking`.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| The title is a heading of a labelled section. | WCAG 1.3.1, 2.4.6 |
| State in words; refund wording follows the payment, not the booking alone. | spec E02 (Refund lifecycle) |
| The mentor's reason is for the mentee; the mentor panel never shows refund details. | spec E02 |
| The page decides the action and its accent; at most one. | R01.5, P5/K3 |

## 8. Content

Titles from spec E02: "Waiting for \{mentor\} — they decide by \{time\}", "Confirmed", "Declined — refund on its way", "Expired — refund on its way", "Refunded", "Refund delayed — we are on it", "Payment not completed — the time was released". Refund sentences say banks take a few days.
