# PendingBookingsNotice

`import { PendingBookingsNotice } from "@devmentor/ui/components/bookings/PendingBookingsNotice"`

Product component, concept `bookings` (E02, #32).

## 1. Purpose

Tells the mentor on `/mentor` that bookings wait for a decision, and by when – the only in-app signal (D15).

## 2. When to use

| Situation in DevMentor | Source |
|---|---|
| Above the checklist on `/mentor` | spec E02, UI/UX |

## 3. When not to use

| Instead of `PendingBookingsNotice`… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A counter badge in the navigation | never | P10 – the number lives in a sentence (Q2) |
| Email | not built | D15: mentors learn in the app only |

## 4. Anatomy

One dense row: "2 bookings waiting for your decision", the nearest deadline (`AcceptDeadline`), *Review bookings*.

## 5. Variants

`emphasis`: the link is the accent, or outline when the page already has its next action.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A labelled list with one row. | WCAG 1.3.1 |
| The count is in words, not a badge. | Q2, P10 |
| One accent per screen: `emphasis` off when the checklist has a next step. | P5/K3 |

## 8. Content

"1 booking waiting for your decision", "3 bookings waiting for your decision".
