# MentorProfileHeader

`import { MentorProfileHeader, MentorProfileHeaderSkeleton, type PublicMentor } from "@devmentor/ui/components/mentors/MentorProfileHeader"`

Product component, concept `mentors` (E01, #28).

## 1. Purpose

Tells a developer who the mentor is and what they have done, on the mentor's share page.

## 2. When to use

| Situation in DevMentor | Source |
|---|---|
| `/m/[slug]`, above prices and times (batch 4 screen) | spec E01, UI/UX |

## 3. When not to use

| Instead of `MentorProfileHeader`… | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A row in the public list | `MentorCard` (batch 4) | dense row, one line of metadata (R01.1) |
| An avatar wall, years of experience | nothing | A05 |

## 4. Anatomy

Region *About the mentor*: headline (section-title size), `TagList`, `PublicWorkLinks`, bio (plain text, line breaks kept). The name is the page title (`PageLayout`).

## 5. Variants

None.

## 6. States

Not interactive (links have their states). Data states: loading (`MentorProfileHeaderSkeleton`), partial (missing parts left out), empty (nothing filled in), long content.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A named region inside `main`; the name is the page's `h1`. | WCAG 1.3.1 |
| Bio is plain text with line breaks – no Markdown. | spec E01 ("bio … plain text") |
| Text lines stay within about 65 characters (`max-w-prose`). | prompt stage 1 (typography) |

<Canvas of={Stories.Region} />

## 8. Content

The mentor's own words. No avatar, no "X years", no ratings (A03, A05). Bio up to 2,000 characters.
