# D03 — Every mentor has a share link; the public list shows only mentors who opt in
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, Arkadiusz (product owner of this repository), confirmed in the om-discover session
- Context and the options weighed: a mentor refused to be listed next to strangers and wants to send her own link (interview 2026-09-03, staff engineer); a mentee once spent 40 minutes finding a mentor and gave up (interview 2026-09-01). Options: share link plus an opt-in list; link only; public list for all.
- Decision and why: each mentor gets a personal share link; appearing on the public list is the mentor's choice. Origin: the agent's recommendation, accepted by the owner – it serves both accounts at a small extra cost.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: a mentee without a known mentor depends on how many mentors opt in to the list.
- Status: active
