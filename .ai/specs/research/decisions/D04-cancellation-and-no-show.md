# D04 — Cancellation and no-show: free until 12 hours before; then the mentor is paid; mentor no-show refunds the mentee the same day
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, Arkadiusz (product owner of this repository), confirmed in the om-discover session
- Context and the options weighed: a mentor wants to be paid when a mentee does not show up ("po to jest przedpłata", interview 2026-09-03); a mentee wants a same-day refund when the mentor does not show up (interview 2026-09-02). Preply allows free cancellation up to 12 hours before a lesson, full cost after (benchmark 2026-09-04). Options: Preply-like rules; always refund the mentee; each mentor sets their own policy.
- Decision and why: the mentee may cancel free of charge until 12 hours before the session; a later cancellation or a mentee no-show pays the mentor; a mentor no-show gives the mentee a full refund the same day. Origin: the agent's recommendation, accepted by the owner. The 12-hour threshold is copied from Preply, not measured with our users.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: needs a way to mark who did not show up; revisit if disputes about no-shows appear.
- Status: active
