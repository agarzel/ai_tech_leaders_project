# D13 — Slots are bookable only from 24 hours ahead; supersedes D09
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, Arkadiusz, confirmed at the Open Questions gate of `.ai/specs/2026-10-19-booking-and-prepayment.md`
- Context and the options weighed: with D09 (bookable from 12 h) and R05/D07 (accept until 12 h before), a booking made 12 h 05 min ahead leaves the mentor five minutes and almost always expires into a refund. Options: keep D09 and measure expiries; bookable from 24 h; auto-accept late bookings (would change D05/R05).
- Decision and why: bookable from 24 h, so the mentor always has at least 12 hours to decide. Same-day help was already not served (D09); a paid-then-refunded booking is the worst mentee experience. Origin: the agent's recommendation, accepted by the owner.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the nearest bookable slot is at least a day away, widening the gap to the urgent need (A01). Revisit with booking data.
