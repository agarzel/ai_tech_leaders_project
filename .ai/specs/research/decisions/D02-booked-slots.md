# D02 — Version 1.0 books a slot in the mentor's calendar; no on-demand "within the hour" help
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, Arkadiusz (product owner of this repository), confirmed in the om-discover session
- Context and the options weighed: mentees described needing help within 20 minutes, within the hour or the same day (interviews 2026-08-20, 2026-09-01, 2026-09-02); the workshop chose 25/50-minute sessions. Options: book a slot; on-demand help within an hour (needs mentors on call, none invited yet); leave it open.
- Decision and why: the mentee picks a free slot published by the mentor. Owner's choice (the agent presented the options without a recommendation); simpler to build and needs no on-call mentors.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the product does not promise immediate help; the earliest slot depends on mentors. Whether developers with an urgent problem accept the nearest slot is untested (see the brief's riskiest assumptions).
- Status: active
