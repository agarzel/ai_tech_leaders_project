# D07 — The mentor must accept the question no later than 12 hours before the session; otherwise the booking expires and the mentee is refunded
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, Arkadiusz (product owner of this repository), confirmed in the om-discover session
- Context and the options weighed: D05 makes the mentor accept the question; the waiting time had to be bounded. Options: until the free-cancellation limit of D04 (12 hours before); decide later.
- Decision and why: acceptance closes 12 hours before the session, the same point as free cancellation in D04, so both sides know where they stand by then. Owner's choice; the 12-hour value comes from D04 (Preply), not from user research.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: a mentee booking less than 12 hours ahead cannot be accepted under this rule – how such late bookings work is an open question.
- Status: active
