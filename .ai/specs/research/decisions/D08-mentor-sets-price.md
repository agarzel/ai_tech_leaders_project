# D08 — Each mentor sets the price of their 25- and 50-minute sessions; the platform commission is decided before launch
- Date, owner: 2026-10-19, Arkadiusz (product owner of this repository), confirmed in the om-discover session
- Context and the options weighed: mentors want to set their own price (interviews 2026-09-03: "zobaczyłbym, co inni mają", "wysoko, żeby bookowali tylko poważni"); the founders' board assumes a 15–20% session commission; Topmate charges 10% through the expert's own link and 20% through its marketplace (benchmark 2026-09-04). Options: mentor sets the price with the commission decided later; mentor price plus a fixed 15%; fixed platform prices.
- Decision and why: mentors set their own prices; the commission is a setting decided before launch. Origin: the agent's recommendation, accepted by the owner.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the commission value is an open question that blocks launch, not the build.
- Status: active
